# Special Action timing and resolution audit

This audit treats the phase printed on each card as a real rules boundary rather than a decorative label.

## Timing contract

### Discussion Phase

Discussion Actions are played openly while planning is still in progress. The card leaves the acting Cognition's hand immediately, its effect becomes visible immediately, and the new state must be available to trading, Strategy legality, contribution previews, and later commitments in the same round.

### Start of Play Phase

Start-of-Play Actions are configured with an ordinary Strategy and remain hidden until simultaneous reveal. They create a conditional commitment. The Special Action resolves first; if its required condition fails, the Special Action and paired Strategy are discarded and the Strategy produces no effects.

An unmet Private Need remains in play. The rulebook retains an unmet Private Need between Situations, so a failed private-targeted Strategy cannot discard the Need itself.

## Card-by-card contract

| Card | Timing | Required interaction | Resolution contract |
|---|---|---|---|
| SA1 Spontaneous Help | Discussion | Choose one unresolved Public Need | Replace it immediately with one newly drawn Public Need using the current Situation setup. The changed board immediately changes legal Strategies and trades. |
| SA2 Deep Introspection | Start of Play | Choose one ordinary Strategy and assign it specifically to the acting Cognition's Private Need | At reveal, check that Strategy against only the acting Cognition's Private Need as its qualification gate. If it does not tend that Need, discard both cards and apply none of the Strategy's effects. If it qualifies, resolve the Strategy normally across all matching Needs. |
| SA3 Group Therapy Session | Discussion | Play openly | Immediately opens a private-targeted commitment option for every Cognition for the remainder of the round. Each Cognition may still play normally instead. A Strategy explicitly assigned to a Private Need must match that Need at reveal or it is discarded without effects. |
| SA4 Emergency Situation | Discussion | Play openly | Draw two unique Need cards immediately and place them as active one-gift Bonus Needs. They may qualify Strategies, affect trades, and score during the current round. |
| SA5 Effective Communication | Discussion | Play openly | Place an active one-gift Understanding Bonus Need immediately. It may qualify Strategies, affect trades, and score during the current round. |
| SA6 Unexpected Turn of Events | Discussion | Play openly | Activate Event effects immediately for every ordinary Strategy for the remainder of the round. Previews, legality, trading, and resolution all use the Event effects. |
| SA7 Deep Breath | Start of Play | Choose one ordinary Strategy and one positive effect printed on it | At reveal, add +3 to the chosen effect before legality and resolution are calculated. |

## Presentation contract

Discussion Actions should no longer be visually attached to an ordinary Strategy during simultaneous reveal because they already resolved openly during planning. The Story Table should distinguish:

- Discussion Actions already active before commitment;
- Start-of-Play Actions resolving at reveal; and
- ordinary Strategies turning over together.

Paired-card layouts must remain wholly inside the phone viewport. A decorative overlapping card may not extend outside the bounded reveal stage.
